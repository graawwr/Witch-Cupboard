# Witch Cupboard — Design Context

## Users

A solo practitioner who keeps an inventory of herbs, candles, jars, crystals, oils and curios on their phone — AND who shares the recipes they build with other witches on Instagram and group chats. The app has two audiences in one:

- **Personal tool** — used casually day-to-day to adjust stock ("I used 2 sprigs of rosemary"), build a working in the Cauldron, and glance at the Grimoire.
- **Community artifact** — the exported recipe card is a giftable, postable image. It needs to look like a plate torn from an illuminated grimoire, not a Canva template.

Primary context of use: at the kitchen counter, at the altar, in the garden, on the couch. Mixed lighting, often daytime. Phone held in one hand.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **illuminated · apothecary · grimoire**.

Voice: warm, unhurried, botanically literate. Slightly archaic ("scribe", "brew", "intention", "gathered", "method") but not caricature. Never corporate, never jokey-TikTok, never tarot-edgelord.

Emotional goal: opening the app feels like opening a tooled-leather journal you've inherited.

## Aesthetic Direction

**Theme: LIGHT parchment with deep, richly illustrated ornament.** The app chrome (nav, lists, buttons) is clean and restrained so the illustrated artifacts (recipe cards, hero banners, empty states) have room to breathe.

Think illuminated manuscript meets art-nouveau apothecary:
- Cream/ivory parchment with visible age spots and warm flecks
- Wrought-iron pillar borders with vines curling around them
- Bronze/copper apothecary pots on wooden shelves
- Bottle-green potions and bell-jars with candle flames
- Hand-painted botanical specimens (leaves, berries, mandrake root)
- Dusty violet, burnt sienna, muted sage, iron-dark pewter, candle-amber
- Symmetrical compositions with heavy marginalia

References:
- Illuminated herbal manuscripts (Leonhart Fuchs, Nicholas Culpeper woodcuts)
- Art-nouveau frames (Mucha, pre-Raphaelite book plates)
- Brian Froud / Alan Lee botanical ink-and-watercolor
- Reference asset folder: `Assets/Untitled design (1)` — hand-illustrated frames, apothecary shelves, iron-pillar ornaments. These ARE the target fidelity; when in doubt, ship illustrated imagery rather than hand-coded SVG.

Anti-references (do NOT look like this):
- Cottagecore / Etsy pastel linen
- Gothic / Victorian mourning (too monochrome, too theatrical)
- Tarot apps with black backgrounds and neon sigils
- Cyan-on-dark SaaS dashboards
- "Magical" apps with purple-to-blue gradients and glowing text
- Minimal Swiss/Notion productivity apps with Inter and white boxes

### Typography

- **Display**: `Unna` (Google Fonts) — handmade warmth with enough drama for a grimoire spread. Used for screen titles, recipe card titles, and section labels on card artifacts.
- **Body**: `Literata` (Google Fonts) — scholarly book face designed for long reading. Used for item names, descriptions, UI copy, and recipe-card body text.
- **Forbidden**: Inter, Roboto, Arial, Open Sans, system-ui defaults, Fraunces, Newsreader, Lora, Crimson family, Playfair Display, Cormorant family, Syne, IBM Plex family, Space Mono, Space Grotesk, DM Sans, DM Serif family, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Instrument Sans, Instrument Serif.
- Fixed `rem` scale. Steps ≥ 1.25 ratio. Vary weight and size for hierarchy.
- On recipe cards (hand-illustrated context) use italic Unna small-caps for section labels like "gathered" and "method".

### Color Palette (OKLCH)

Warm parchment neutrals (~85° hue), paired with a richer accent set than typical UI apps. Rarity discipline still holds — accents are earned, not sprinkled.

```
/* Parchment — tinted toward warm ochre */
--parchment-0:   oklch(0.95 0.022 82);   /* page */
--parchment-1:   oklch(0.91 0.032 82);   /* base surface */
--parchment-2:   oklch(0.86 0.040 78);   /* raised surface */
--parchment-3:   oklch(0.76 0.050 72);   /* well-loved, aged */

/* Ink — iron-gall warm brown-black */
--ink:           oklch(0.25 0.035 55);
--ink-muted:     oklch(0.42 0.030 50);
--ink-soft:      oklch(0.58 0.025 50);

/* Iron pewter — near-black accent with violet undertone */
--pewter:        oklch(0.30 0.020 285);
--pewter-deep:   oklch(0.22 0.020 285);

/* Copper / bronze — primary warm accent */
--copper:        oklch(0.55 0.110 55);
--copper-deep:   oklch(0.45 0.120 50);

/* Bottle-green — potion glass, deep forest */
--bottle:        oklch(0.42 0.095 155);
--bottle-deep:   oklch(0.32 0.090 155);

/* Candle-amber — warm glow, used for "active" accents */
--candle:        oklch(0.78 0.120 78);
--candle-deep:   oklch(0.62 0.140 62);

/* Dried-violet — pressed flowers, rare */
--violet-dried:  oklch(0.55 0.090 315);

/* Oxblood — destructive only */
--oxblood:       oklch(0.42 0.120 25);
```

**Accent hierarchy:** copper is the default primary (CTAs, active tabs). Bottle-green is secondary (moss/natural contexts — herb chips, success). Candle-amber is the attention accent (badges, toasts, unread). Violet and oxblood are rare.

### Spacing

Semantic 4pt scale: `--space-2xs 4`, `--space-xs 8`, `--space-sm 12`, `--space-md 16`, `--space-lg 24`, `--space-xl 32`, `--space-2xl 48`, `--space-3xl 64`.

### Motion

Calm, paper-like. Exponential easing (`ease-out-quart/quint/expo`). No bounce, no elastic. Animate only `transform` / `opacity`. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Design Principles

1. **Illustrated artifacts do the heavy lifting.** The recipe card and hero banners are hand-illustrated images. UI chrome (nav, lists, buttons) stays clean and serves the artifacts, never competes with them.
2. **Parchment, not plastic.** Surfaces read as aged paper with warmth and texture. Never glossy cards with generic drop shadow.
3. **Ink and copper earn their weight.** Rich accents (copper, bottle, candle) stay rare — 10% of the surface at most. Overuse kills them.
4. **The recipe card is the hero.** It's designed to be screenshot and shared. Full-bleed illustrated frame, room for content in the parchment window.
5. **Every word smells of the garden.** Warm, slightly archaic copy: "scribe", "brew", "gathered", "method". Never "✨ Magic ✨", "Oopsie!", "Cast spell", "Conjuring…".

## Absolute CSS bans

- `border-left` / `border-right` > 1px as a colored stripe on cards, callouts, or list items.
- `background-clip: text` with any gradient (gradient text).
- Glassmorphism used decoratively on every panel.
- Generic rounded-rectangle-with-drop-shadow card patterns.
- Bouncy / elastic easing.
